The Box

Everything you need to put together the perfect gift.

What goes inside the box

Contents

🃏

5 printed destination cards

Start the Game, Japan, Italy / Europe, Uruguay, Final Choice. Print from the Print Cards page.

✉️

5 envelopes

One per card. Label each envelope clearly so she knows the order to open them.

📝

A handwritten letter

The letter template is below. Write it by hand — it matters more than anything else in the box.

🎁

A small travel gift

Something that feels like travel: a beautiful passport holder, a luggage tag, or a mini globe.

🎤

Wireless karaoke microphone Optional

She loves singing. This is the kind of gift she would actually use and smile every time she does.

🌍

Mini globe or passport holder Optional

A physical reminder that the adventure is real and coming. Passport holders are especially meaningful for a travel gift.

How to label the envelopes

Envelope Order

  1. Start Here — the game card. She opens this first and scans the QR code.
  2. Japan — the Japan destination card.
  3. Italy / Europe — the Italy/Europe card.
  4. Uruguay — the Uruguay card.
  5. Final Choice — open this when she's ready to decide.

Add a small note on top of the box or on the outside:

"Open the Start the Game card first. Then explore each destination. When you are ready, choose where we go next."

The handwritten letter

Template

For your 38th: Choose Our Next Adventure

Happy birthday, my love.

You love travelling. You love singing. And I love watching you come alive through both.

This year, I did not want to give you just another thing. I wanted to give you a choice, a little game, and the beginning of our next adventure.

There are three dreams waiting for you: Japan, Italy or Europe, and Uruguay. Each one feels different. Each one could be beautiful. But the best part is the same in every version: I get to go there with you.

You have become such a beautiful mother to Kabir, and I love watching that part of you. But I also want you to always remember that you are still you. The woman I fell in love with. The woman whose voice, laugh, and heart make life feel bigger. The woman I would travel anywhere with.

So play the game. Explore the cards. Choose the place your heart wants most.

I will plan it.

Happy 38th birthday. I love you.

One last thing

The best part of this gift is not the box, the cards, or the trip.
It's the moment she realises you planned all of this for her.
That's the real gift.
