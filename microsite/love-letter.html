A letter I've been meaning to write

For your birthday. For the trip. For everything.

June 2026

always ♡ B,

I love you a lot.

every day I'm trying my best every day to become better. A better partner, a better husband, and the person you deserve.

I don't know how many times I can say it, and it will still never be enough — but the amount of love and support you give our family means everything. You are our foundation. I can't imagine my life without you.

this is true You have always, always, always put me, my things, what I want, and what I need ahead of everything you want. You are so selfless. And I want to give back to you.

a promise So my promise for this birthday is that we will make Spain happen. We will both be capable people, able to run around, walk around, and enjoy it properly. It will happen. There may be a couple of stops on the way — but your dream of living in Spain, I will make that true. Just trust me on that.

And we will do it in style. We will do it the way you really want to do it. Not living randomly, trying to figure things out, or worrying about everything. We are going to live the way you want to live in Spain, because I am going to make sure that is the life you deserve.

Kabir I know Kabir can't say it yet — but as a mother, if God had written the definition of mother, you would be that definition.

You are so harsh on yourself. You are so hard on yourself, and I think that is totally unfair. You should take the time you need. You should go meet your friends. You should do what you need to do. Let's take care of your health. Go out for Pilates. Take time for yourself.

I mean it I will try to do more. I will try to be there more. I know I should probably help you more with cooking so you have more time in the evening to do your things. We will work on these things this year, so you have more time for yourself — which you totally deserve.

But for now, this is your 38th birthday. We are going to have an amazing party.

I love you so much. Kabir loves you so much.

Thank you for everything you do.

All my love,
