Only for you

A letter I've been meaning to write

For your birthday. For the trip. For everything.

June 2026

for you ♡ Happy birthday, my love.

I've been thinking about what to write for weeks. Not because I don't have enough to say — because I have too much, and I wanted to get it right. So I'm going to try.

this one You are 38 years old today, and I want you to know something: you are more beautiful, more alive, and more you than you have ever been. I watch you every day — with Kabir, with your music, with the quiet moments you don't think anyone notices — and I think: how did I get this lucky?

You love travelling. You love singing. And I love watching you come alive through both. There is a version of you that only shows up when you are somewhere new, or when you are singing without knowing anyone is listening. I live for that version. I want to give her more room this year.

Kabir too Kabir is watching us, you know. He is watching how we love each other. He is watching what a marriage looks like when it's full of laughter, adventure, and real tenderness. You are teaching him what love looks like just by being you.

This birthday gift is not about the destination. It never was. It's about giving you a moment where the whole decision belongs to you. Where I stop, step back, and say: where do you want to go? What do you want to feel? What does your heart need? And then I make it happen.

always Wherever you choose — Japan, Italy, Uruguay — I already know my favourite part: I get to go there with you. That's the whole point. That's always been the whole point.

So choose. Take your time. And then trust that I will make it everything you dreamed, and a little more.

I love you. More than I say. More than you probably believe on an ordinary Tuesday. But it's true on every ordinary Tuesday too.

Always yours,
Your husband ♡
